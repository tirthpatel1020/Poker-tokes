# The Toke Book

An hour-by-hour tip ledger for poker dealers. Log what you make each hour;
it works out the hourly floor top-up, tracks what the cage takes, handles
flat-rate tournament hours, and shows you which hours of the night actually pay.

Everything is stored on your own device. No account, no server, no data leaves
your phone. Use **Download CSV** for a backup.

## Put it on your phone

1. Open the site in Safari
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**

It launches full screen like a normal app and works with no signal.

## Setup

Open the **Setup** tab and set your cash floor ($/hr), tournament rate,
and what the cage takes. Then choose whether the number you type each hour
is what you pocket (after the cage cut) or the amount before it comes out.

## Files

- `index.html` — the whole app
- `sw.js` — service worker, makes it work offline
- `manifest.json` — home screen name and icon
- `*.png` — icons
